# Requirement traceability and reconciliation

These standards adopt confirmed product and teaching decisions, with code and tests used to describe implementation. The [master spec v1.0](../Enhtor_Product_Experience_Behavior_Master_Spec_v1.0.md), [curriculum v1.1](../Python_AI_Interview_Learning_Curriculum_v1.1.md), [accepted amendments and engineering decisions](../architecture/DECISIONS.md) and [teaching guidelines](../teaching-guidelines.md) are the public sources. Later approved milestones are cited by milestone and corroborated where possible by code and tests. A reported usability problem explains why a decision was made. It does not establish a general empirical claim or a rule beyond what was approved.

**Order of authority.** Newest explicit owner-approved policy and ADR amendments take precedence over conflicting historical delivery or behavior commitments. The product spec governs experience and the curriculum governs educational intent and sequence. The teaching guidelines and these standards apply those sources. Code describes behavior and limitations. When a consequential ambiguity has no accepted resolution, record it in [Open decisions](OPEN_DECISIONS.md). Preserve the original documents.
